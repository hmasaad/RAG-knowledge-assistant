# Salon Booking App — Requirements Specification

## 10. Notifications

Push booking notifications. Payment details and invoices live in a later section.

## 11. MVP Scope

1. Customer registration/login and profile.
2. Salon discovery and search.
3. Salon details and service catalog.
4. Stylist selection.
5. Availability and appointment booking.
6. Booking history and cancellation/rescheduling.
7. Push booking notifications.
8. Basic salon dashboard and calendar.
9. Service and staff management.
10. Basic admin management.
